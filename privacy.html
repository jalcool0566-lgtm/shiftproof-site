Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Data Not Collected

ShiftProof is designed to operate 100% locally on your device. We do not require an account, do not operate user-tracking servers, and do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information, work schedules, pay rates, or paystub photos.

2. On-Device OCR & Storage

  • Schedule & Paystub Scanning: Images selected from your Photo Library or Files app are processed strictly on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Images never leave your device.
  • Local Storage: Shifts, pay profiles, and paystub audit entries are stored in a local file inside your app container. Deleting the app removes this local data.
  • Calendar Access: Optional Apple Calendar sync only runs when you tap "Sync Cal" and writes only to the local Apple Calendar you approve.

3. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed directly by Apple via StoreKit. ShiftProof never receives your credit card number, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

4. Contact

Questions about privacy? Open a support ticket at github.com/jalcool0566-lgtm/shiftproof-site/issues.
