How does FLSA Blended Overtime work?

Under federal FLSA rules (29 CFR § 778.109), non-discretionary shift differentials (night, weekend, charge, preceptor) are included in your total straight-time wages for each 7-day workweek before dividing by hours worked to find your blended regular rate. Overtime hours above 40 receive an additional 0.5x of that blended rate.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Rules → Upgrade / Manage → Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID.

Does ShiftProof connect to my hospital or Kronos account?

No. ShiftProof never asks for your employer login. Schedule and paystub screenshots are scanned privately on your phone using Apple Vision.
